Before you start

This site connects you with random strangers over live video, audio and text. You may see or hear things that are inappropriate or upsetting.

  • You must be 18 or older.
  • No nudity, harassment or sharing personal info. Use Report if someone breaks this.
  • Video, audio and chat go straight between you and the other person. They can see your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer call.
  • Your browser will ask for camera and mic access. You can still chat by text if you say no.
