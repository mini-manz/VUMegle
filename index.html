Welcome to VUMegle

Meet random people over live video, audio and text. Press Start, say hi, and press Next whenever you want someone new. You may see or hear things that are inappropriate or upsetting.

The rules

  • 18+ onlyYou must be 18 or older to be here.
  • Be kindNo harassment, hate or bullying.
  • Keep it cleanNo nudity or sexual content.
  • Stay privateDon't share your name, address or other personal info.

Good to know

  • Video, audio and chat go straight between you and the other person and aren't saved. On a direct call they can see your IP address, as with any peer-to-peer call.
  • Your browser will ask for camera and mic access. You can still text chat if you say no.
  • Someone breaking the rules? Press Report, then Next.
  • Keyboard: press Esc to start, and press it twice to stop.
